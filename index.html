COMMANDO Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 | Effective Date: Immediate

1. Overview & Commitment

COMMANDO Men's Luxury Atelier ("COMMANDO", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and personal data. This privacy document outlines how we collect, store, process, and protect your information when utilizing our iOS application and concierge ordering services in full compliance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines.

2. Data Collection & Purpose

We strictly limit collection to essential information required to deliver luxury tailored menswear to your destination:

  • Account & Contact: Full name, phone number, and verified Gmail address used strictly for authentication and order coordination.
  • Delivery Location (GPS): Used solely while actively using the app (When In Use) to pinpoint your exact courier delivery address. We never track your location in the background.
  • Authentication & Storage: Encrypted tokens managed via Supabase infrastructure with Row-Level Security (RLS) enforcement.
  • Profile Photos (Optional): Camera/Photos access is requested only if you choose to set an avatar profile picture.

3. Third-Party Integrations & Security

  • Supabase: Encrypted database and authentication backend secured with strict Row Level Security.
  • Google Sign-In & Sign in with Apple: Secure single sign-on mechanisms adhering to Apple Guideline 4.8.
  • Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to data brokers or third-party advertising networks.

4. Account Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

You maintain full control of your account. Users may delete their account and all associated profile records directly within the app at Account > Settings > Delete Account. Initiating deletion permanently removes your personal profile, addresses, and stored preferences from both local device storage and our servers.
